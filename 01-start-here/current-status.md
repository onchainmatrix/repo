# Current Status

Onchain Matrix is being deployed in phases. Documentation should clearly separate infrastructure that is available or in launch-stage operation from product layers that remain planned.

## Launch-stage / current focus

The current protocol focus is:

- **Treasury formation** and establishment of the protocol-owned capital base.
- **ONMX token infrastructure** on BNB Chain.
- **Seed access and token-sale infrastructure**, subject to eligibility and applicable terms.
- **Multi-signature treasury control and security procedures.**
- **Initial treasury deployment and treasury-policy implementation.**
- **Public documentation, contract verification, and transparency standards.**

## Activation stage

The treasury engine is designed to expand toward:

- structured allocation across approved strategies,
- event- and threshold-based rebalancing,
- risk-controlled deployment,
- treasury reporting and performance dashboards,
- increasingly automated monitoring and execution.

## Roadmap components

The following should be treated as **planned protocol layers unless and until publicly launched and verified**:

- pool-based collateralized lending,
- P2P structured credit,
- tokenized debt issuance,
- secondary-market liquidity for approved debt positions,
- broader RWA-backed credit integrations,
- expanded credit-market fee and servicing infrastructure.

## Source of truth

Publicly deployed contracts, official protocol interfaces, published security reports, and the live Terms should take precedence over descriptive roadmap language if there is ever a conflict.

> Roadmap timing is illustrative and can change based on security readiness, legal requirements, market conditions, liquidity, technical validation, and treasury scale.
