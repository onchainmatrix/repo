# ONMX Token Overview

**ONMX** is the ecosystem alignment token of Onchain Matrix.

* **Network:** BNB Chain
* **Standard:** BEP-20
* **Fixed total supply:** 1,000,000,000 ONMX

ONMX is designed around long-term alignment with treasury growth, protocol utility, value capture, and protocol sustainability.

## Economic design

The token model is built around several principles:

### Fixed supply

ONMX does not rely on ongoing inflationary emissions as the core source of protocol yield.

### Treasury formation

A significant portion of supply is allocated to treasury formation and long-term protocol capital. This supports the productive capital base used by the treasury and future protocol infrastructure.

### Protocol utility

ONMX utility can include protocol participation, fee-related utility or discounts in supported products, and governance or control functions where explicitly implemented.

### Revenue-linked value capture

Protocol surplus can support treasury growth, buybacks and burns, and eligible stablecoin distributions where permitted and approved under protocol policy.

These mechanisms are not guaranteed and can depend on treasury performance, market conditions, protocol policy, legal requirements, and available liquidity.

## No inflationary yield promise

Onchain Matrix is designed so that treasury yield and protocol revenue come from productive capital deployment and protocol activity, not from minting additional ONMX as a recurring reward source.

## Rights and terms

The legal and economic rights associated with ONMX are defined by the applicable contracts, protocol documentation, and participation terms.
