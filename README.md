# Welcome to Onchain Matrix

**Capital infrastructure for onchain finance.**

Onchain Matrix is a treasury-driven protocol designed to connect protocol-owned treasury management, productive capital deployment, collateralized credit, tokenized debt markets, and ONMX-aligned protocol economics within one capital framework.

The protocol is being developed on **BNB Chain** with a capital-preservation-first approach. Treasury capital is treated as productive protocol capital: it can be allocated across approved crypto, stablecoin, RWA, yield, and future credit strategies while operating under defined risk, security, and execution controls.

## Core system

Onchain Matrix is organized around three connected layers:

1. **Treasury & Yield Engine** — forms, preserves, allocates, and grows protocol-owned capital.
2. **Collateralized Credit & Tokenized Debt** — extends treasury infrastructure into structured credit, lending liquidity, transferable debt positions, and secondary-market activity.
3. **ONMX Ecosystem Alignment** — connects protocol activity, treasury growth, fee utility, revenue allocation, buyback-and-burn mechanics, and eligible stablecoin distributions without relying on inflationary ONMX emissions.

## Design principles

* Preserve capital before pursuing return.
* Generate yield from productive deployment, not token emissions.
* Fund operations from yield and protocol revenue rather than routinely spending treasury principal.
* Separate financial risk management from cybersecurity controls.
* Use multi-party control, controlled upgrades, monitoring, and public onchain verification for critical infrastructure.
* Introduce credit and tokenized-debt functionality in phases as the protocol, treasury, security controls, and market infrastructure mature.

> Some components described in this documentation are live or in launch-stage deployment, while others are roadmap features. See **Current Status** for the distinction.
