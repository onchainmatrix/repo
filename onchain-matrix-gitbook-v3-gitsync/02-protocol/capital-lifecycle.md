# Capital Lifecycle

Onchain Matrix treats treasury capital as long-duration protocol infrastructure.

## 1. Capital formation

Capital formation establishes the productive treasury base. At the launch stage, seed, presale, public-sale, and treasury-reserve allocations are structured to support treasury formation and market liquidity rather than routine operating burn.

## 2. Capital preservation

Preservation requires more than holding stable assets. It includes:

- reserve liquidity,
- diversification,
- strategy and protocol caps,
- collateral quality requirements,
- oracle and market-depth controls,
- custody and key security,
- controlled upgrade and emergency procedures.

## 3. Capital growth

Treasury growth is intended to come from productive deployment and protocol revenue. Potential return sources include approved yield strategies, credit participation, and future marketplace activity.

The protocol does not rely on new ONMX emissions as the core source of yield.

## 4. Capital deployment and credit extension

As treasury scale, security, and liquidity mature, capital can support broader infrastructure such as:

- collateralized credit,
- tokenized RWAs,
- structured financing,
- tokenized debt,
- secondary-market liquidity.

## Reinforcing loop

The target system is a reinforcing loop rather than a one-time raise:

**capital formation → deployment → revenue → reserve and treasury growth → greater deployable capacity → broader protocol activity.**

The loop remains subject to risk limits. More treasury capital does not automatically imply more risk exposure.
