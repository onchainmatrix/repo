# Supply, Allocation & Vesting

ONMX has a fixed total supply of **1,000,000,000 tokens**.

## Allocation

| Category | % of Supply | Tokens |

## Core Treasury Allocation — 87.5%

The protocol identifies **87.5% of total supply** as the Core Treasury Allocation:

**72.5% Treasury Reserve & LP + 10.0% Public Sale + 2.5% Presale + 2.5% Seed = 87.5%.**

This allocation is intended to support treasury formation and a protocol-owned capital base designed for long-term deployment across approved crypto assets, RWAs, liquidity, yield strategies, and future credit activity.

The classification describes the intended economic use of these allocations. It does not mean all 87.5% is immediately circulating or simultaneously deployed.

## Treasury Reserve & LP lock structure

The 72.5% Treasury Reserve & LP allocation is intended to use a long-duration, third-party vesting or lock structure through **Magna** over an **eight-year** period.

The objective is to provide independent infrastructure and long-duration supply discipline without presenting a fixed public schedule of future treasury sales. Release and deployment mechanics remain subject to the applicable lock structure, treasury policy, market conditions, protocol needs, and published terms.

## Seed vesting

Seed allocations are structured with a **6-month cliff followed by 18 months of linear vesting**, with monthly claim availability under the applicable vesting implementation.

## Other allocations

Final vesting, unlock, custody, and transfer rules for each allocation should be read together with the applicable token-sale terms, vesting contracts, and public onchain records.
