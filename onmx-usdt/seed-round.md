# Seed Round

The seed round is designed primarily to form the initial protocol-owned treasury base. Operations, infrastructure, security, liquidity support, and ecosystem growth are intended to transition toward funding from treasury-generated yield and protocol revenue rather than routine drawdown of principal.

## Seed terms

| Term                              | Current structure                                                   |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Round                             | Seed                                                                |
| Seed allocation                   | 2.5% of total ONMX supply                                           |
| Seed tokens                       | 25,000,000 ONMX                                                     |
| Seed price                        | $0.14 per ONMX                                                      |
| Public-sale / TGE reference price | $0.20 per ONMX                                                      |
| Reference discount                | 30% to the $0.20 public-sale / TGE reference price                  |
| Vesting                           | 6-month cliff + 18-month linear vesting                             |
| Claims                            | Monthly after the cliff under the applicable vesting implementation |
| Network                           | BNB Chain                                                           |
| Primary use of funds              | Treasury formation                                                  |
| Seed capacity                     | $3.5M                                                               |

## Treasury treatment

Capital raised is intended to form and preserve the productive treasury base. Treasury capital can then be deployed under the allocation and risk frameworks described in this documentation.

## Participation and payment

Seed participation is permissioned and subject to eligibility, jurisdictional restrictions, wallet controls, applicable terms, and technical availability. Supported payment assets and networks are defined by the live seed interface and transaction terms.

## Vesting purpose

**Vesting infrastructure**

\
Seed vesting and participant claims are intended to be managed through Magna, powered by Kraken, once production onboarding is active. Participants will be able to monitor their own ONMX allocation, vesting progress, and claimable balance through the Magna stakeholder dashboard. The production vesting contract and current Magna allocation state determine the live claimable balance.
