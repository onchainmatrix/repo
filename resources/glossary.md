# Glossary

## Allocation

The percentage or value of treasury capital assigned to a particular asset, strategy, risk sleeve, liquidity reserve, or protocol function.

## Allocation Limit

A maximum permitted exposure to an asset, strategy, borrower, protocol, collateral type, risk tier, or other defined category.

## Approved Strategy

A treasury strategy that satisfies the protocol's current security, liquidity, risk, market-depth, operational, and other applicable requirements.

Approval does not mean a strategy is risk-free.

## Available Liquidity

Treasury capital that is readily accessible for reserves, deployment, liquidity requirements, or risk response without requiring a material unwind of longer-duration positions.

## Base Yield Strategy

An approved strategy that prioritizes durability, liquidity, security, and capital preservation over maximizing headline yield.

## Borrower

A participant or entity that receives capital under an approved credit position and becomes subject to the applicable collateral, repayment, maturity, and resolution terms.

## Burn

The permanent removal of ONMX from usable token supply through a verifiable onchain mechanism.

## Buyback

A protocol-directed purchase of ONMX using approved protocol resources.

Buybacks are conditional and are not guaranteed.

## Capital Formation

The process of building the protocol-owned treasury base through approved capital inflows.

## Capital Lifecycle

The connected process of forming, preserving, growing, and redeploying capital across the protocol.

## Circuit Breaker

A protective control designed to restrict, pause, or alter activity when predefined risk or security conditions are met.

## Collateral

Assets pledged or locked to secure a credit position.

Approved collateral must satisfy the applicable liquidity, valuation, oracle, market-depth, legal, custody, and risk requirements.

## Collateralized Credit

Credit in which repayment obligations are supported by assets pledged or locked as collateral.

## Core Crypto Allocation

Treasury exposure to approved major crypto assets that can support strategic market exposure and productive capital deployment.

## Core Treasury Allocation

The ONMX supply categories associated with treasury formation and launch capital flows.

The current structure combines:

* 72.5% Treasury Reserve & LP
* 10.0% Public Sale
* 2.5% Presale
* 2.5% Seed

Total: **87.5% of maximum ONMX supply**.

## Credit Position

An approved financing arrangement with defined principal, collateral, maturity, economic terms, repayment conditions, and resolution rules.

## Debt Ceiling

The maximum amount of credit exposure permitted for a borrower, collateral type, pool, strategy, market, or other defined risk category.

## Deployed Capital

Treasury assets actively allocated to approved strategies, positions, liquidity, or other productive uses.

## Deployment Engine

The protocol layer responsible for routing approved treasury capital into permitted assets and strategies under defined allocation and risk controls.

## Emergency Response Control

A security mechanism or operational procedure that can restrict activity, pause defined functions, or otherwise reduce potential damage during an incident.

## Fixed Supply

A token supply model in which the maximum number of tokens is capped.

ONMX has a fixed maximum supply of **1,000,000,000 tokens**.

## Gross Protocol Revenue

Protocol-generated revenue before approved operating expenditures and other protocol-directed uses.

Capital raised for treasury formation is not Gross Protocol Revenue.

## Liquidation

The process of resolving all or part of a collateralized position when defined risk thresholds or default conditions are breached.

## Liquidation Threshold

The collateral or LTV level at which the applicable credit rules permit or require liquidation or another defined resolution action.

## Liquidity Risk

The risk that an asset or position cannot be exited, sold, redeemed, or transferred at an expected price or within an expected time.

## Loan-to-Value (LTV)

A measure of debt relative to the value of collateral securing the position.

**LTV = Outstanding Debt ÷ Collateral Value**

Higher LTV generally means less collateral protection against adverse price movement.

## Market Depth

The amount of executable market liquidity available around current prices without causing excessive price impact.

## Maximum Supply

The maximum number of tokens permitted to exist under the token's supply rules.

Maximum supply should not be confused with circulating supply.

## Multi-Signature / Multi-Sig

A wallet or control structure that requires multiple authorized approvals before defined actions can be executed.

## Net Revenue

Protocol revenue remaining after the defined operating costs or other deductions for the relevant reporting period.

The exact calculation should be identified when the metric is published.

## ONMX

The ecosystem alignment token of Onchain Matrix.

ONMX is deployed on BNB Chain and has a fixed maximum supply of 1,000,000,000 tokens.

## Oracle

A data system used to provide external information to onchain applications, such as asset prices or other market data required for valuation and risk controls.

## Oracle Risk

The risk that incorrect, stale, manipulated, unavailable, or poorly designed data causes improper valuation, execution, or liquidation.

## P2P Matching

A credit model in which lenders and borrowers can be matched around agreed or structured terms rather than relying exclusively on a shared liquidity pool.

## Pool-Based Liquidity

A model in which capital is supplied to a shared liquidity pool that can support standardized borrowing or other defined protocol activity.

## Protocol-Owned Treasury

Capital economically controlled by the protocol and managed under treasury policy for preservation, productive deployment, liquidity, growth, and protocol infrastructure.

## Protocol Revenue

Revenue generated through protocol activity, such as treasury yield, credit activity, fees, servicing, pools, or marketplace activity.

Capital contributed through treasury-formation activity is not protocol revenue.

## Public Sale / TGE Reference Price

The documented transaction or target reference price used in Seed terms to compare the Seed price with the planned Public Sale / TGE structure.

A reference price is not a guarantee of future market price.

## Realized P\&L

Profit or loss that has become economically final through a completed sale, repayment, liquidation, settlement, or other closing transaction.

## Realized Yield

Income or return that has been received, settled, or otherwise economically realized by the treasury.

## Rebalancing

Adjusting treasury allocations in response to allocation drift, liquidity requirements, changing yield conditions, risk changes, market events, or other policy triggers.

## Revenue Allocation

The process of directing protocol revenue among approved uses such as operations, risk reserves, treasury growth, buybacks, burns, eligible stablecoin distributions, or protocol expansion.

## RWA / Real-World Asset

An offchain economic asset or financial exposure represented or accessed through tokenized or onchain infrastructure.

RWAs can introduce issuer, custody, pricing, legal, liquidity, settlement, and redemption risks in addition to smart-contract risk.

## Seed Round

The permissioned ONMX round designed primarily to form the initial protocol-owned treasury base.

The current documented Seed allocation is 25,000,000 ONMX, representing 2.5% of maximum supply.

## Secondary-Market Liquidity

Market infrastructure that can allow an eligible position or tokenized debt instrument to be transferred or traded after origination.

Availability of secondary liquidity is not guaranteed.

## Stablecoin & Liquidity Base

The treasury sleeve intended to preserve maneuverability, support liquidity requirements, and maintain lower-volatility capital available for approved protocol activity.

## Stablecoin Distribution / Airdrop

A protocol-directed distribution of eligible stablecoin value to qualifying participants where permitted and implemented.

Stablecoin distributions are not guaranteed and remain subject to legal, policy, eligibility, treasury, and operational conditions.

## Structured Credit

Credit established under predefined economic, collateral, maturity, repayment, monitoring, and resolution terms.

## TGE

**Token Generation Event.**

The launch-stage event associated with the initial public availability or market activation of a token under the applicable launch structure.

## Timelock

A control that creates a defined delay between approval of an action and its execution.

Timelocks can provide time for review, monitoring, or protective action before high-impact changes take effect.

## Tokenized Debt

An onchain representation of an approved debt or credit position.

Where implemented, tokenization can support transferability, fractionalization, accounting, and approved secondary-market liquidity.

## Treasury Principal

Capital contributed to and retained as part of the productive protocol treasury base, distinguished from generated yield, protocol revenue, and market gains.

## Treasury Reserve & LP

The 72.5% ONMX allocation designated for long-term treasury reserve and liquidity purposes under the applicable supply, lock, custody, and treasury policies.

## Treasury Value

The estimated fair value of assets and positions economically controlled by the protocol treasury at a stated reporting timestamp.

## Unrealized P\&L

Changes in the estimated value of an asset or position that have not yet been finalized through sale, repayment, settlement, or another closing transaction.

## Vesting

A release mechanism under which token access becomes available over time according to predefined conditions or a schedule.

## Warning Zone

A defined risk level that occurs before liquidation or another critical threshold and can trigger monitoring, restrictions, collateral adjustments, or other protective actions.

## Yield

Economic return generated by productive capital deployment.

Yield should be distinguished from new capital entering the treasury and from unrealized changes in asset prices.
