# Roadmap & Execution Plan

Onchain Matrix is designed to scale in phases, beginning with treasury formation and progressing toward treasury-engine activation, credit infrastructure, tokenized debt markets, and broader ecosystem expansion.

## Phase 1 — Treasury Formation & Launch

Focus:

- seed and launch-stage treasury formation,
- token and sale infrastructure,
- liquidity setup,
- multi-signature treasury control,
- security review and audit process,
- public contract verification,
- initial treasury deployment,
- documentation and transparency standards.

## Phase 2 — Treasury Engine Activation

Focus:

- structured allocation,
- approved yield strategies,
- treasury reporting,
- treasury growth,
- event- and threshold-based rebalancing,
- risk-controlled capital deployment,
- monitoring and automation maturity.

## Phase 3 — Credit Platform Rollout

Focus:

- pool-based liquidity,
- P2P matching,
- collateralized borrowing,
- lender onboarding,
- structured credit terms,
- collateral and LTV controls,
- borrower and exposure limits,
- utilization growth.

## Phase 4 — Tokenized Debt & Market Expansion

Focus:

- transferable debt positions,
- approved secondary-market liquidity,
- tokenized RWA integrations,
- broader credit-market activity,
- additional infrastructure and market integrations.

## Timing

Roadmap timing is intentionally conditional. Security validation, treasury scale, legal readiness, liquidity, market conditions, third-party dependencies, and technical testing can change sequencing or launch dates.

The roadmap should be read as an execution plan, not a guarantee of delivery by a fixed date.
