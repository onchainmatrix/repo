# Community Airdrops

### Rewarding Participation, Not Farming

Onchain Matrix is designed to recognize genuine, sustained ecosystem participation rather than temporary activity, task farming or short-term balance manipulation.

The protocol may periodically consider discretionary stablecoin airdrops for eligible community participants. These airdrops are intended to complement the ONMX ecosystem without introducing inflationary token emissions.

### Non-Inflationary Framework

If an airdrop is approved:

* No new ONMX will be created to fund it.
* Treasury principal will not be used.
* Capital reserves, operating requirements, risk buffers, taxes, fees and other obligations will be accounted for first.
* The airdrop may be funded only from available protocol resources after those requirements have been satisfied.
* The amount available may vary between periods or may be zero.

Onchain Matrix may review airdrop availability quarterly. A quarterly review does not guarantee that an airdrop will be approved or conducted.

### Time-Weighted Participation

Potential eligibility may consider an eligible wallet’s time-weighted ONMX balance over a defined measurement period.

Unlike a single wallet snapshot, time weighting considers both:

* The amount of eligible ONMX recorded; and
* The length of time that balance was maintained.

This approach reduces the benefit of acquiring ONMX immediately before an eligibility date and gives greater recognition to sustained participation.

There is no permanent minimum holding period or guaranteed eligibility formula. The measurement period, qualifying balances, excluded wallets and other conditions may be established separately for each approved airdrop.

### Eligibility

Eligibility may be subject to:

* Wallet and participant verification;
* Geographic and jurisdictional restrictions;
* Sanctions and compliance screening;
* Applicable campaign terms;
* Technical verification of eligible balances;
* Exclusion of Treasury Reserve, team, advisor, protocol-controlled, liquidity-management and other designated wallets; and
* Any additional legal, tax or regulatory requirements.

Transferring ONMX to another wallet does not automatically transfer the previous wallet’s participation history. Administrative wallet changes may be reviewed where ownership can be independently verified.

### No Guaranteed Benefit

Community airdrops are discretionary and are not an automatic feature or guaranteed benefit of holding ONMX.

Holding or acquiring ONMX does not:

* Guarantee participation in an airdrop;
* Guarantee any payment, return or fixed APY;
* Create a contractual right to protocol revenue; or
* Create a claim over the Treasury Reserve or its underlying holdings.

The timing, frequency, eligibility criteria, funding source and amount of any airdrop may be changed, postponed, suspended or discontinued.

Where an airdrop is approved, its final terms and eligibility requirements will be published separately before execution, subject to applicable legal and compliance requirements.
