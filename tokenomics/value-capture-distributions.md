# Protocol Revenue & ONMX Value Capture

Onchain Matrix is designed to generate protocol revenue from multiple activities rather than a single yield source.

## Potential revenue streams

### Treasury yield

Revenue generated from approved treasury strategies and productive capital deployment.

### Credit spread participation

Protocol revenue generated from borrowing activity, lender participation, or treasury participation in structured credit markets.

### Origination & servicing fees

Fees that can be generated when credit positions are created, serviced, monitored, transferred, or resolved.

### Pool & marketplace activity

Revenue associated with lending pools, secondary-market activity, and tokenized debt transactions.

## Revenue allocation

Protocol revenue can be allocated among four broad priorities:

### Treasury growth

Retaining part of surplus revenue expands the productive capital base and can increase future deployment capacity.

### Buyback & burn

Where policy, treasury conditions, market liquidity, and legal considerations permit, surplus revenue can be used to repurchase ONMX. Repurchased ONMX may be burned, linking protocol surplus to supply reduction.

Buybacks are not guaranteed and should not impair treasury resilience.

### Stablecoin Airdrop

Eligible stablecoin-based airdrop can be used where permitted and implemented. These distributions are designed to avoid creating ONMX inflation, but they remain subject to legal, policy, eligibility, treasury, and operational constraints.

### Operations & risk reserves

Revenue can fund infrastructure, security, development, liquidity support, risk reserves, and ecosystem expansion so that routine operations do not depend on repeatedly spending treasury principal.

## Economic objective

The intended model is:

**real protocol revenue → treasury growth + operating capacity + disciplined value capture**, rather than **new token emissions → temporary yield**.
