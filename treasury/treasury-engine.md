# Treasury & Yield Engine

The treasury is the protocol's productive capital base. It is designed to preserve capital, generate recurring yield, and support long-term protocol growth.

## Treasury principle

**The treasury is not intended to function as routine operating capital.**

The operating model is designed so that infrastructure, security, liquidity support, development, and ecosystem growth can increasingly be funded from treasury-generated yield and protocol revenue rather than by systematically spending down treasury principal.

## Three core functions

### Capital allocation

Treasury capital can be allocated across approved assets and strategies based on:

- risk,
- liquidity,
- return potential,
- security history,
- market depth,
- counterparty or protocol exposure,
- portfolio concentration.

### Yield generation

Productive deployment is intended to create recurring yield without relying on inflationary ONMX emissions. Yield targets are subordinate to treasury preservation and risk controls.

### Rebalancing and controls

Allocation rules, exposure limits, liquidity needs, and market conditions guide how capital can be adjusted over time.

## Treasury assets

The treasury framework can support a diversified mix that may include:

- stablecoins and liquidity reserves,
- BTC, ETH, BNB, and other approved major crypto assets,
- approved onchain yield strategies,
- tokenized RWAs,
- approved collateralized credit participation.

Specific holdings and allocation weights can change over time under treasury policy.

## Why the treasury matters

A larger, well-managed treasury can increase deployable capital, deepen protocol liquidity, support future credit activity, and create additional revenue capacity. Treasury size alone is not the objective; **risk-adjusted productivity and resilience** are.
